I am a Ph.D. student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Renjing Xu. I received my B.Eng. in Robotics Engineering from Shenzhen University.

My research is on robot learning: visual representations that capture how the world moves, manipulation policies that learn from limited demonstrations, and whole-body control for legged manipulators.
